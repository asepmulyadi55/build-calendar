Two different things can be bought here, and they are refunded differently. This page says plainly which is which.

## Coins are non-refundable

Coins are **non-refundable** and cannot be converted back to cash. They are also non-transferable between accounts.

This is stated before purchase, on the pricing page and at checkout, because it is the term most worth understanding before you spend anything.

Two things soften it, and both are deliberate:

- **The preview is free.** You can build a whole calendar, look at every sheet, and decide you do not want it — without spending a coin.
- **Coins never expire.** If you top up and change your mind about this year's calendar, the balance is still there next year.

## When a coin is not charged at all

A coin is only charged once your first export has actually succeeded. If an export fails because something went wrong on our side — a render error, a storage problem, a timeout — no coin is deducted. If one was already taken, it is returned automatically.

You do not need to ask for this and you do not need to notice it happened.

## Printed orders

Printing is bought separately and never uses coins.

Refunds on a printed order cover **production defects** only: wrong trim, colour that does not match the file we printed from, binding that fails, damage in transit, or a sheet we printed incorrectly.

We do not refund a printed order because the design turned out differently from what you pictured. The preview shows exactly what will be printed, which is why we ask you to look at it before ordering.

To claim, message us on WhatsApp within **7 days** of delivery with photographs of the problem. We will reprint or refund.

## Wrong charge

If you were charged an amount you did not authorise, or a top-up was verified twice, contact us. Payment mistakes are corrected in full — that is a different thing from a refund, and it is never subject to the rules above.
