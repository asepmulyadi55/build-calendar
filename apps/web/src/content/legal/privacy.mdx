This page explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do. It is written plainly because a policy nobody reads protects nobody.

## What we collect

- **Your account**: email address, and a name if you give one.
- **Your calendars**: the design, the text you type, and the photos you upload.
- **Payments**: the amount, the method, and the proof image you upload for manual verification. We never see or store your card or bank credentials.
- **Basic technical logs**: error records used to fix faults.

## Your photos

Photos you upload are used for one thing: producing your calendar.

- **We strip EXIF metadata, including GPS coordinates**, when we process an upload. Family photos routinely carry the location they were taken — often a home address — and that data does not need to survive to print a calendar.
- We keep the resized versions we need for preview and print, and discard the original once they exist.
- We never use your photos as public samples without asking you first, in writing.
- Your photos are not used to train anything and are not sold or shared for advertising.

## Who else touches your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service:

- A managed database and authentication provider, for your account and your designs.
- Object storage, for photos and exported files.
- An email provider, for verification and password reset messages.
- An error-monitoring service, which receives fault reports without your photos.

Each holds only what it needs to do its job.

## How long we keep it

- Your calendars and files stay while your account is open, so you can re-download them.
- Payment records are kept as long as accounting requires.
- Deleted accounts have their photos and calendars removed. Ledger entries are kept in anonymised form, because they are financial records.

## Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your data. Message us on WhatsApp or by email and we will confirm when it is done.

Note that deleting your account removes your calendars and files. There is no way to get them back afterwards.

## Cookies

We use only what is needed to keep you signed in. There is no advertising tracker on this site.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so on the site before the change takes effect.
